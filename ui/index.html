Insurance for businesses that run without a person in the loop.

An autonomous business can buy, sell and move money. It cannot buy insurance: underwriting needs a legal person, and a claim needs an adjuster — so a loss is simply absorbed. Overcover writes cover against an objective public index and settles it from contract rules. No underwriter. No claims queue.

  • Cover is defined, not negotiated — the trigger, premium and payout are fixed when a product is published, and cannot change afterwards.
  • The payout is reserved from the pool the moment cover is bought, so a promise is always backed by capital already held.
  • The oracle's word is challengeable — a reading is signed, verified on-chain, and can be disputed with a bond.

How a policy works, end to end

Eight steps. Each is a real contract rule; the actor who triggers it is named.

  1. A product defines the risk

    The owner publishes a city, an index, a trigger threshold, a premium rate, payout bounds and a capacity cap. It is immutable afterwards.

    owner · add_product

  2. A provider funds the pool

    Anyone deposits NEAR and becomes an underwriter. Their capital earns the premiums and can only be withdrawn while it is not reserved.

    capital provider · deposit

  3. A business buys cover

    It pays the premium the product fixes, for a payout between the product's bounds, on an observation date. The payout is reserved from the pool immediately.

    policyholder · buy_cover

  4. The attestor publishes a signed reading

    A value for the index on the observation date, signed with ed25519. The contract verifies the signature, the freshness and a single-use nonce before accepting it.

    attestor · report_observation

  5. The reading can be challenged

    During the challenge window anyone can dispute a reading by posting a bond and a counter-value. A disputed reading cannot settle a policy.

    anyone · challenge_observation

  6. The owner resolves the dispute

    Upholding the reading forfeits the challenger's bond into the pool. Rejecting it nullifies the reading and returns the bond.

    owner · resolve_dispute

  7. The policy settles

    Once the window has elapsed, settlement pays the beneficiary iff the validated reading meets the threshold. Either way the reservation is released, and it can happen only once.

    anyone · resolve

  8. The result is verifiable

    The payout, the reservation release and the pool identity are all contract state. Any observer can reproduce every figure on this page from a public RPC endpoint.

    anyone · get_pool, reconcile
