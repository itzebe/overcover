Why the promise holds

Three rules enforced in the contract, not in a policy document.

Risk is published, not negotiated

A product fixes the index, the trigger, the premium, the payout bounds and a capacity cap. Once published it cannot change, so a buyer cannot re-price cover after the fact.

Payout is reserved at purchase

Buying cover locks the payout out of the pool immediately. The solvency invariant is re-checked after every mutation, so the pool can never promise more than it holds.

The oracle's word is challengeable

Readings are ed25519-signed and verified on-chain. Anyone can dispute one by posting a bond; the loser's bond is forfeited into the pool.
